
\begin{question}
In an inspection of 200 units, 27 defective units were found. The fraction defective is \(p=0.135\).

Which interpretation is correct?
\begin{answerlist}
  \item Exactly 86.5\% of future units will be conforming.
  \item The process is automatically in statistical control.
  \item Approximately 13.5\% of the inspected units were defective.
  \item The process capability index equals 0.135.
\end{answerlist}
\end{question}

\begin{solution}
The fraction defective is the proportion of defective items in the inspected sample.
\end{solution}
