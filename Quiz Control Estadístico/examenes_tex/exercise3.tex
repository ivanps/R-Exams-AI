
\begin{question}
A Shewhart control chart has center line 106, UCL = 118 and LCL = 96. A new sample statistic is 120.

What is the best conclusion?
\begin{answerlist}
  \item The process is out of statistical control.
  \item The process is centered and capable.
  \item The process variability has decreased.
  \item No action is needed; the point is within limits.
\end{answerlist}
\end{question}

\begin{solution}
The plotted point exceeds the UCL, indicating a special cause and loss of statistical control.
\end{solution}
