
\begin{question}
A process has \(C_p=1.7\) and \(C_{pk}=1.4\).

Which conclusion is most appropriate?
\begin{answerlist}
  \item The process is out of statistical control.
  \item The process spread is adequate, but the process is not perfectly centered.
  \item The specification limits are wider than the process spread because \(C_{pk}>C_p\).
  \item The process is perfectly centered between specifications.
\end{answerlist}
\end{question}

\begin{solution}
When \(C_{pk}<C_p\), the process has potential capability but is shifted away from the target or center.
\end{solution}
