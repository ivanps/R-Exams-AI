
\begin{question}
A quality-control inspector examines 10 products. Each product has a probability of 0.30 of being defective, independently of the others.

Let \(X\) denote the number of defective products.

What is the probability that \textbf{exactly 4 products are defective}?
\begin{answerlist}
  \item 0.1503
  \item 0.8497
  \item 0.2001
  \item 0.6496
\end{answerlist}
\end{question}
