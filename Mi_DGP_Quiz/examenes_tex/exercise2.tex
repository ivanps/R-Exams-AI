
\begin{question}
A random sample of size \(n=30\) was obtained from a population. The sample mean was \(\bar{x}=65\) and the sample standard deviation was \(s=10\).

Compute the standard error of the sample mean using

\[
SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}.
\]

Round your answer to two decimal places.
\end{question}

\begin{solution}
Using

\[
SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}},
\]

we obtain

\[
SE(\bar{x})=
\frac{10}{\sqrt{30}}
=
1.83.
\]
\end{solution}
