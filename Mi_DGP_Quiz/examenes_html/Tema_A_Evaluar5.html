  2. Question

    A random sample of size n=30n=30 was obtained from a population. The sample mean was x‾=65\bar{x}=65 and the sample standard deviation was s=10s=10.

    Compute the standard error of the sample mean using

    SE(x‾)=sn. SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}.

    Round your answer to two decimal places.


    Solution

    Using

    SE(x‾)=sn, SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}},

    we obtain

    SE(x‾)=1030=1.83. SE(\bar{x})= \frac{10}{\sqrt{30}} = 1.83.
