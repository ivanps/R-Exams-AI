  2. Question

    A random sample of size n=35n=35 was obtained from a population. The sample mean was x‾=60\bar{x}=60 and the sample standard deviation was s=9s=9.

    Compute the standard error of the sample mean using

    SE(x‾)=sn. SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}.

    Round your answer to two decimal places.


    Solution

    Using

    SE(x‾)=sn, SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}},

    we obtain

    SE(x‾)=935=1.52. SE(\bar{x})= \frac{9}{\sqrt{35}} = 1.52.
