  2. Question

    A random sample of size n=35n=35 was obtained from a population. The sample mean was x‾=73\bar{x}=73 and the sample standard deviation was s=11s=11.

    Compute the standard error of the sample mean using

    SE(x‾)=sn. SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}.

    Round your answer to two decimal places.


    Solution

    Using

    SE(x‾)=sn, SE(\bar{x})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}},

    we obtain

    SE(x‾)=1135=1.86. SE(\bar{x})= \frac{11}{\sqrt{35}} = 1.86.


  3. Question

    The following figure shows a normal distribution with mean μ=55\mu=55 and standard deviation σ=5\sigma=5. The shaded region represents P(X≤47.5)P(X \le 47.5) for a normally distributed random variable XX.

    Compute the probability represented by the shaded area. Round your answer to four decimal places.


    Solution

    The required probability is

    P(X≤47.5)=Φ(47.5−555). P(X \le 47.5) = \Phi\left( \frac{47.5-55}{5} \right).

    The corresponding probability is

    P(X≤47.5)=0.0668. P(X \le 47.5) = 0.0668.
